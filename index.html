Abstract

Deep research agents are increasingly capable of searching the open web and synthesizing evidence, yet they remain poorly suited to controllable report generation in multimodal deep research: handling interleaved textual and visual inputs, gathering multimodal evidence through multi-step web search, and delivering comprehensive illustrated reports under user-specified requirements. Existing systems underuse images discovered along the search trajectory and produce fixed-form answers with limited control over report granularity, multimodal composition, and evidence grounding. We introduce MIRA, a Multimodal Illustrated Deep Research Agent for controllable reports. At its core, MIRA-Harness translates user requirements into generation contracts and connects four stages: evidence collection, report submission, feedback-driven revision, and final delivery. Building on this harness, MIRA-Data constructs high-quality multi-hop multimodal research trajectories, and we post-train foundation models to operate within the workflow. Using Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, MIRA achieves state-of-the-art performance among open agents on 7 representative, established multimodal-search and deep-research-agent benchmarks, while producing user-aligned illustrated reports with traceable textual and visual evidence.
